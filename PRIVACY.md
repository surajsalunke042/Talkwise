# Talkwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Talkwise is an Android app for practising spoken English with an AI conversation partner called Wise. It is developed by Suraj Salunke ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Contact: surajds042@gmail.com

## In short

- You do not create an account. Talkwise never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
- Your conversation text is sent to Google's Gemini models to produce Wise's replies.
- Your level, streak and saved words are stored online under a random anonymous ID.
- There are no ads, and I do not sell your data.
- You can erase everything from inside the app at any time.

## What the app collects

**Anonymous ID.** When you first open the app, Firebase Authentication creates a random identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your name or to any other account.

**Learning progress.** Stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your anonymous ID:

- your level and your vocabulary, grammar and fluency scores
- your daily goal, streak, and how many minutes and turns you practised each day
- the words you save to your word pocket, with their meanings
- a summary of each session: the scenario, its length, the number of turns, and the short recap Wise wrote for you

The full text of your conversations is **not** stored in this database.

**Conversation text.** What you say or type, together with the most recent turns of the conversation and your current level, is sent to Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic so that Wise can reply. At the end of a session, up to the last 40 lines are sent once more to write your recap. The app keeps the conversation in memory only while the session is open.

**Your voice.** In voice mode the app uses the microphone, only while you are in a conversation and have tapped the mic. Talkwise does not record or store audio. Your speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service installed on your device (on most phones, Google's). Depending on your device settings, that service may send the audio to its provider's servers to recognise it.

**Wise's voice.** Replies are read aloud by your device's text-to-speech engine. When an online voice is used, the text of the reply is sent to that engine's provider to be turned into speech.

**Reports.** If you tap "Report this reply", the reported reply, the message of yours that came just before it, and the scenario are stored under your anonymous ID so that I can review them.

**Technical data.** The Firebase services the app relies on (Authentication, Firestore, Remote Config, App Check with Play Integrity, and AI Logic) receive technical information such as your IP address, device model, operating system version and an installation identifier. This is used to deliver the service and to protect it from abuse.

## What the app does not collect

Talkwise does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or advertising ID. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

## How the information is used

- to hold a conversation with you and correct your English
- to estimate your level and adapt Wise to it
- to keep your streak, goal and saved words
- to review reported replies and improve the app
- to keep the service secure and within its usage limits

## Who receives it

Your data is processed by Google, which provides the Firebase and Gemini services Talkwise is built on. Google handles it under its own terms and privacy policy:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Gemini API terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms


Google may use the conversation text sent to Gemini to improve its products, and human reviewers at Google may read it. For this reason, please do not share personal, confidential or sensitive information in your conversations with Wise.

I do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## Where it is stored and for how long

Your data is stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country. It is sent over encrypted connections.

Your progress, saved words, session summaries and reports are kept until you delete them. Uninstalling the app does not delete them, because the app can no longer tell the server who you were; delete your data first, or contact me.

## Your choices

- **Delete your data.** On the home screen, tap **Delete my data**. This erases your progress, saved words, session summaries and reports, and deletes your anonymous ID.
- **Remove a saved word.** Open the word pocket and remove it.
- **Use the app without the microphone.** Switch to keyboard mode, or refuse the microphone permission. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your device settings.
- **Ask me.** If you cannot use the app any more, email me and I will help. Because the app is anonymous, I may not be able to find your data without your installation's ID.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your data, such as access, correction or objection. Contact me to use them.

## About Wise's replies

Wise is an AI. Its replies, corrections and scores are generated automatically and can be wrong or inappropriate. If a reply is offensive or incorrect, tap **Report this reply** under it or in the transcript.

## Children

Talkwise is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact me and I will delete the data.
